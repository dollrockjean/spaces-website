Design Studio

Where your home stops being an idea.

Picking materials at big-box stores is overwhelming. At the Spaces Design Studio you sit down with your own designer, see your home in 3D and hold the real samples in your hands. Many clients choose everything in one visit. Others take it room by room. Either way, nothing is ordered until you sign off.

  • Cabinetry
  • Hardware
  • Flooring

  • Countertops
  • Tile & backsplash
  • Plumbing fixtures
  • Paint & trim

A visit, step by step

  1. Step 1

    Bring your ideas

    Saved photos, a Pinterest board or just a feeling. Every starting point works.

  2. Step 2

    See it in 3D

    Walk through a model of your rooms before anything is built.

  3. Step 3

    Hold the real thing

    Cabinets, hardware, floors, lighting, counters and tile, all in one room.

  4. Step 4

    Sign off with confidence

    You approve every choice and its price before we order a thing.
